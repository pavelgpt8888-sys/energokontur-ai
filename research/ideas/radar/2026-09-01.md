# Energy Technology Radar — 2026-09-01

Fresh signals only; repeated themes are included only where there is a material new commercial/technical fact.

## A-tier — immediate due diligence

### 1. Quantum sensing stack for substations and distribution grids
- Status: RESEARCH / LOCALIZE
- China: Hefei 220 kV Houdian quantum demonstration substation has operated since Nov 2024 with 18 categories / 85 devices covering quantum measurement, communications and computing.
- Commercialization signal: March 2026 launch of a full 'Jingwei' product family covering measurement, metering, monitoring, warning and encrypted communications.
- Reported field results at Wuxi distribution demo: reliability 99.98%, PV utilization >95%, manual inspection workload -60%.
- Particularly interesting modules: diamond-NV quantum current sensors, quantum-dot gas sensors for early cable/switchgear fire/PD warning, quantum tilt sensors, quantum-secure gateways.
- Business angle: do not attempt full quantum stack. Identify 1–2 sensor classes with measurable advantage over conventional CT/PD/gas sensing and evaluate Chinese OEM/licensing plus local gateway/software.

### 2. Standardized digital 132 kV substation design as a productized engineering system
- Status: LOCALIZE
- DEWA reports 90 substations built using its standardized digital design, 23 commissioned in H1 2026.
- Reported benefit per substation: >2 months shorter design/execution; ~AED 5m lower cost; ~8,000 electronic parts and 140 km copper wiring eliminated; footprint -220 m²; annual energy reduction equivalent to 182 MWh.
- Business angle: not a new primary device, but a repeatable engineering/product platform. Candidate for BY/RU: template-based digital substation design library + IEC 61850 architecture + standardized panels/BOM + digital commissioning tooling.

### 3. 6.5 kV SiC MOSFET moving into sampling/production
- Status: WATCH / COMPONENT OPPORTUNITY
- NoMIS Power demonstrated >8 kV blocking, 90 mΩ Rds(on), 55 A on a 6.5 kV large-die SiC MOSFET.
- Sampling in 2026; standard production targeted Q4 2026. 3.3 kV family already in production; roadmap extends to 10 kV MOSFETs and 20 kV SiC IGBTs.
- Business angle: medium-voltage converters can reduce series device count and converter complexity. Track Chinese analogues, modules, packaging, gate drivers, insulation and cooling rather than chip fabrication.

### 4. Superconducting shunt reactor for compact urban substations
- Status: RESEARCH / WATCH
- China put into operation a 10 kV / 1 Mvar air-core toroidal HTS shunt reactor at Shanghai Gulong substation.
- Footprint <6 m², noise <60 dB, near-zero spatial leakage field; plug-and-play integration claimed.
- Shanghai Superconductor supplied ~50 km of REBCO tape.
- Business angle: niche urban retrofit where land/noise/EM field constraints dominate. Main uncertainty is REBCO + cryogenic lifecycle economics.

### 5. Live-line robotic maintenance platform — utility-backed commercialization
- Status: OEM CANDIDATE / RESEARCH
- Japan's hibot + Italian TSO Terna have >2 years collaboration; Terna Forward invested in hibot in Dec 2025.
- Platform travels on energized transmission conductors/ground wires and performs maintenance, not only inspection.
- Earlier field tests installed preformed rods and marker balls; CIGRE 2026 joint showcase signals movement toward operational adoption.
- Business angle: tooling/adapters and localized maintenance workflows may be a better entry than building robots from scratch.

## B-tier — promising but narrower / longer horizon

### 6. Graphene-modified Cu-W arc contacts for high-voltage breakers
- Status: RESEARCH
- Chinese academic work on 40.5 kV breakers shows graphene/Cu-W contact composition materially affects erosion and contact resistance; 2026 publications describe improved erosion mechanisms.
- Vendor claims >20% conductivity and arc-ablation-resistance improvement should be treated as unverified until independent type/lifecycle data are found.
- Business angle: contact material/powder metallurgy could be a high-margin localization niche if lifetime extension survives independent testing.

### 7. Advanced composite conductors — new India commercial order
- Status: OEM CANDIDATE / RESEARCH
- Himachal Pradesh State Electricity Board selected TS Conductor AECC for a turnkey reconductoring project in Aug 2026.
- Vendor claims 2–3x transmission capacity vs traditional conductors while retaining standard installation practices.
- Business angle: reconductoring existing rights-of-way is becoming a major global capex category; need China supplier map, installed-cost comparison and mechanical/icing suitability for BY/RU.

### 8. Iron-air / zinc / flow LDES moving from pilot to project finance
- Status: WATCH
- Invinity won a 43 MWh vanadium-flow order from a U.S. utility in Aug 2026.
- Eos zinc-based LDES has an initial $100m order for Blanquilla Phase 1 and record backlog; Form Energy's iron-air commercial pipeline exceeds 750 MW / 75 GWh and it raised a large 2026 financing round.
- Business angle: not import-ready for BY/RU today, but strong proof that 8–100 h non-lithium storage is becoming a bankable equipment category.

## C-tier / signals

### 9. Ultra-deepwater 525 kV HVDC cable system
- Status: WATCH
- Nexans qualified a complete 525 kV MI HVDC cable + joints for 3,000 m installation depth; mechanical test load reached 842 kN.
- Important for deep-sea interconnectors/offshore, but low near-term relevance to Belarus; Russia relevance only for specialized offshore corridors.

### 10. SF6-free transmission breakers/GIS continue extending upward
- Status: WATCH / COMPONENT OPPORTUNITY
- GE Vernova launched 420 kV / 63 kA CO2/O2 live-tank breaker and 170 kV SF6-free GIS at CIGRE 2026.
- This is not a new direction for our radar, but the new 63 kA/420 kV product materially extends the operating envelope.
- Business angle: monitor Chinese/Korean alternatives and subcomponents (seals, gas handling, vacuum interrupters, monitoring).

## Top due-diligence targets
1. Quantum current / gas / tilt sensors for power systems — isolate one commercially defensible sensor class.
2. Standardized digital-substation engineering platform — calculate BY/RU savings from copper, cabinets, footprint and engineering time.
3. 6.5 kV SiC supply chain — identify Chinese devices/modules and converter architectures enabled by fewer series devices.
4. Live-line robotic tooling — focus on task-specific end effectors and retrofit to existing robotic platforms.
5. Graphene Cu-W contacts — verify independent lifecycle/type-test economics before considering localization.
