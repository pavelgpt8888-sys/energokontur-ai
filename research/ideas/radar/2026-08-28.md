# Energy Technology Radar — 2026-08-28

## Strong new candidates

1. **Oil-impregnated densified wood (ODW) transformer insulation** — RESEARCH / MATERIAL LOCALIZATION
   - Nanoengineered densified wood veneer with isolated 1D oil nanochannels.
   - 3.5× tensile strength vs high-density oil-impregnated paper, 1.6× through-plane thermal conductivity, >70% tensile strength retained after 6 weeks at 150°C; demo transformer ran ~10°C cooler.
   - Potential route: license/process transfer/local manufacture for transformer insulation and later dry transformers/motors.
   - Source: https://techxplore.com/news/2026-06-nanoengineered-wood-insulation.html

2. **Medium-voltage UPS for AI/data centers and heavy industry** — RESEARCH / OEM SEARCH
   - GE Vernova launched a true MV UPS on 2026-08-24, installed between facility MV supply and critical loads, combining power conversion, controls and storage.
   - Protects larger load blocks than low-voltage UPS and buffers sudden load swings before they hit utility/gas-turbine supply.
   - Shipments expected mid-2027, first energization late-2027.
   - Search Chinese OEM analogues rather than GE resale.
   - Source: https://www.gevernova.com/news/press-releases/ge-vernova-introduces-medium-voltage-ups-help-accelerate-buildout-ai-factories

3. **Grid Intelligence Node for low-voltage feeders using broadband PLC/BPL** — OEM CANDIDATE / LOCALIZE
   - Corinex launched GIN on 2026-08-24: near-real-time current, voltage and power-quality measurement + event detection, with communications over the power line.
   - Installs in secondary substations, street cabinets and branches; avoids building a separate communications network in some cases.
   - Product hypothesis for BY/RU: feeder visibility node + local analytics for overloaded phases, voltage quality, available capacity and fault/event detection.
   - Source: https://www.corinex.com/news/corinex-launches-grid-intelligence-node-to-reveal-the-true-state-of-the-low-voltage-grid

4. **Mingyang MCD5.0 ultra-low-wind-speed 5 MW turbine** — RESEARCH / OEM / BY SITE ECONOMICS
   - First unit completed 2026-08-21/26. Cut-in 2.5 m/s; 7.74 m²/kW swept area; reported 1,850 equivalent full-load hours at 4.5 m/s average wind.
   - Drivetrain component count reduced 60%; modular replacement of large nacelle components without large lifting crane; claimed +10% reliability / generation gains depending on source.
   - Potential relevance to low-wind inland markets such as Belarus, but must validate local wind-resource LCOE before considering import.
   - Source: https://www.windtech-international.com/product-news/mingyang-completes-first-5-mw-ultra-low-wind-speed-turbine

5. **Live switchgear operation robot, up to 220 kV** — OEM / RESEARCH
   - State Grid Jiaxing system first applied in 2021, now promoted across Zhejiang; performs switching on energized switchgear.
   - Reported 84.7% efficiency increase per bay; 19 invention patents.
   - Russia has a 45.2m-ruble development project for a 110-kV substation diagnostic/switching robot, so this is not a free market but Chinese maturity may create OEM/technology-transfer opportunities.
   - Sources: https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/m/china/2026-03/09/c_1166256.htm ; https://www.cnews.ru/news/top/2025-08-18_krupnejshij_v_rossii_energeticheskij

6. **Drone-assisted direct power-line de-icing** — IMPORT / OEM CANDIDATE
   - Chinese utilities are moving drones from inspection to physical intervention; 2026 operations used UAVs with insulating tools to remove ice from lines in mountainous regions.
   - Product hypothesis for BY/RU: de-icing attachment + industrial UAV + safe operating procedure, potentially cheaper/faster than ground access in difficult terrain.
   - Source: https://en.people.cn/n3/2026/0302/c90000-20430141.html

7. **Dry-wet synergistic SO3 removal retrofit for coal plants** — RESEARCH / RU-FOCUSED
   - China Power Pu'an completed retrofit of two 660 MW units in March 2026; first domestic application of dry-wet synergistic SO3 removal.
   - Intended to reduce blue plume and air-preheater fouling; NOx and SO2 hourly averages fell 50% after upgrade.
   - Relevant mainly to Russian thermal generation and large industrial boilers; Belarus potential smaller.
   - Source: https://www.chinapower.hk/en/media/news-p260320.php

8. **Haffner H6 biomass thermolysis to stable syngas** — RESEARCH / LOCAL FEEDSTOCK ECONOMICS
   - Independent qualification milestone announced 2026-08-06. H6 is redesigned for simpler mechanics and >8,000 h/year operation.
   - Converts biomass to syngas for downstream renewable methane, methanol, hydrogen, biochar/biogenic CO2 value chains.
   - Potential BY angle: wood/agricultural residues, but only if feedstock logistics and product offtake beat direct combustion/biogas economics.
   - Source: https://www.haffner-energy.com/key-qualification-milestone-h6-generation-technology/

9. **16 MW TLP floating wind directly powering offshore oilfield** — WATCH / RU-OFFSHORE
   - CNOOC's Haiyou Anlan began operation 2026-08-06. 16 MW, ~136 km offshore, expected 54m kWh/year, typhoon-resistant, direct oilfield power via subsea cable.
   - Tension-leg platform reduces steel use and sea-area footprint vs semi-submersible concepts.
   - Not a Belarus opportunity; potential long-term Russia offshore/oil-gas component opportunity.
   - Source: https://www.cnooc.com.cn/English/press_328/newsreleases/202608/t20260810_122293.html

10. **Encomara Squid universal floating-wind mooring/power interface** — WATCH / COMPONENT OPPORTUNITY
   - Received ABS TRL 7/8 qualification on 2026-08-27 after inshore trials.
   - 40-tonne interface supports moorings, power cables and subsea connectors above seabed, enabling pre-installation and rapid turbine hook-up/disconnection.
   - Potential value is lower offshore construction risk and easier tow-to-port maintenance.
   - Source: https://oceannews.com/news/energy/encomaras-squid-connector-earns-trl-7-8-qualification-from-abs-for-floating-wind-deployment/

11. **High-altitude airborne wind S4000** — WATCH
   - China completed full-process flight/power/recovery test at 4,000 m altitude in August 2026.
   - 67×40×23 m helium platform, 20-year design life, intended for desert/highland/island power supply and grid connection.
   - Engineering-stage technology; economics, helium losses, tether reliability, aviation regulation and storm survival remain major unknowns.
   - Source: https://gxt.hunan.gov.cn/gxt/xxgk_71033/gzdt/dfgz/202608/t20260824_34049564.html

12. **Isolated directly-cooled 1.2–3.3 kV SiC discrete package** — COMPONENT WATCH / OEM SEARCH
   - Navitas UHV-TO-247-4-ISO integrates >6 kV isolation and direct-cooled thermal path for 1.2, 2.3 and 3.3 kV SiC MOSFETs.
   - Targets grid PCS, BESS, SST and high-power data-center conversion; could reduce external isolation/thermal-management complexity.
   - Search Chinese packaging analogues and local power-module assembly opportunities.
   - Source: https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/navitas-introduces-isolated-through-hole-package-sic-mosfets-enabling-direct-cooled

## Current Top-5 for deeper due diligence
1. ODW transformer insulation.
2. Low-voltage Grid Intelligence Node / feeder visibility.
3. Ultra-low-wind 5 MW turbine economics for Belarus/Russia.
4. Medium-voltage UPS — especially Chinese OEM analogues for data centers and continuous-process industry.
5. Drone direct-intervention/de-icing tools for power lines.

## Red-team notes
- Robotics is advancing, but Russia already has active domestic substation-robot development; do not assume a blank market.
- MV UPS is not a new category globally (ABB already sells HiPerGuard up to 24 kV); GE's novelty is the AI-factory architecture and grid/load buffering positioning. Treat as a market opportunity, not a scientific breakthrough.
- Low-wind turbine economics depend on real capacity factor, logistics, grid connection and financing; a 2.5 m/s cut-in speed alone is not an investment case.
- S4000 airborne wind has impressive engineering validation but still lacks bankable LCOE/availability data.
