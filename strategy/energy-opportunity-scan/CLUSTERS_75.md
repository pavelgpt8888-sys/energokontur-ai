## What to do with this taxonomy
1. Do not score every feature as a separate product.
2. Shortlist `Pain × Frequency × WTP × Repeatability × buyer accessibility`, deduct integration/support/sales/regulatory friction.
3. For a customer, select one end-to-end process, **not** an AI buzzword.
4. Measure baseline and quote audit/pilot/managed service only against a defined scope.
5. Keep safety-critical grid/OT actions human-approved and outside first autonomous MVPs.
