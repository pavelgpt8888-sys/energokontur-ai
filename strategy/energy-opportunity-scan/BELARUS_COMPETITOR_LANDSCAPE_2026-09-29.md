# Competitive landscape Belarus: AI automation & industrial B2B

**Research date:** 2026-09-29. Public-information screen / first wave, not comprehensive registry or estimate of all Belarus IT companies.

## Counting rule

Included a business if there is a publicly accessible BY-facing service/portfolio offer or a current vendor-partner listing. Count each offer family once (e.g. PRORESULT / bit24.by). Excluded unverified geo-landing pages of foreign providers, pure AI API infrastructure without an implementation offer, and repeated products of one company.

**In this curated evidence set: 33 distinct offer families / providers: 18 advertising direct AI/BPM/agent automation, 15 adjacent CRM/ERP/ECM/RPA/industrial IT incumbents.** This is a *documented lower bound for discoverable relevant providers*, not total number of competitors active in Belarus. Service page presence ≠ verified customer success or qualified implementation team. Direct and adjacent are classification of offers, not market share.

## Four findings that directly revise our proposed strategy

### 1. The concept "process audit → AI pilot → production → support" is **already standard public positioning**
- [AIAI.BY](https://aiai.by/consulting) offers process audit, AI roadmap, technology choice and ROI.
- [A2 Consulting](https://a2c.by/services/ai-rpa-ml/) offers process/data audit, KPI-led AI/RPA/ML and integration to enterprise systems.
- [YYTECH](https://yytech.by/) offers 1–2 week AI audit, 4–6 week pilot, rollout/SLA.
- [FS](https://fs.by/service/vnedrenie-iskusstvennogo-intellekta/) offers audit, architecture, API deployment and metric review.
- [Whale Studio](https://whale.by/vnedrenie-aiii-i-avtomatizaciya) explicitly positions itself as AI layer over existing ERP/CRM/WMS/documents.

**Implication:** Do not position ourselves as “we implement AI, calculate ROI, integrate with 1C and support” alone. The differentiation must be a *specific economic outcome and operational domain*, with proof. Our 10–12k BYN audit hypothesis requires defined customer scale, 2–3 scoped processes, baseline, opportunity calculation, integration/security feasibility and decision-grade roadmap. It is not a locally validated market-clearing fee.

### 2. Two original “unique” energy/industrial ideas already have public analogues
- YYTECH [detailed case](https://yytech.by/): electro-technical manufacturing, PDF source, substation quotation, engineer review, vendor-reported 5–10 min vs 2 hours; ventilation equipment PDF selector for PTO.
- A2 [projects](https://www.a2c.by/projects/): RAG-based tender application check and manufacturing QA/ML.

**Implication:** PDF-to-specification, “AI tender reader” and basic technical quotation are feature candidates, not defensible company positioning. Need test a longer scope:
`RFQ → technical verification → supplier price/cost → quote approval → order → stock/production status → margin → invoice/payment exception`.
This may still have other competitors; no claim of whitespace is made.

### 3. CRM/ERP incumbents own the data-entry point
- [Первый Бит](https://www.1cbit.by/services/rpa-robotizatsiya-biznes-protsessov/) offers robots for bank statements, reconciliations and 1C; [local Bitrix offer](https://www.1cbit.by/avtomatizatsiya-biznes-protsessov-bitriks24/).
- [1C ERP partner directory](https://1c.by/firm1c/partner/erp/) lists YuKOLA, MiSoft, Glavbuh, Human Intel System etc.
- [1C implementation registry](https://1c.by/solutions/?sort=arm) includes SoftService ↔ Grodnoenergo ERP (Oct 2024), Electronic Economy ↔ Minskenergo document/reference/purchasing system (Feb 2021).

**Implication:** A fresh standalone CRM or ERP would fight existing installations. Better integrate, co-sell with such providers or work on approved cross-system exceptions / paid outcomes. Treat them as both substitutes and potential channels.

### 4. Entry-level AI build prices are already visible and low
Published *starting* examples; not comparable complex enterprise scopes or independent median:
- [Deadline](https://deadline.by/uslugi/ai): RAG chatbot from **2,000 BYN**, AI agent from **3,500 BYN**.
- [R9 BPM](https://r9.by/business-process-automation): start/process/custom BPM examples from **900 / 1,800 / 3,600 BYN**.
- [R9 AI](https://r9.by/ai-agents-automation): AI website chatbot from **1,800**, AI+CRM from **3,500**, enterprise ecosystem from **6,000 BYN**.

**Implication:** Competing on “we build a bot/agent” is a price-comparison game. Pricing a 10–12k discovery must have a much deeper, independently useful management decision deliverable than a quick configuration estimate. Cheapest or published “from” fee does not constrain complex industrial pricing.

## Revised product exploration

**Reject simplistic differentiators:**
- “Our automation uses AI, n8n, Python or open source.”
- “We speak director's language and calculate ROI.”
- “We add agents above 1C/Bitrix.”
- “We offer audit → pilot → integration.”
These are already public messaging of several players.

**Three narrower paid hypotheses** (need sales validation):
1. **Industrial Quote-to-Cash Operations:** a managed function from technical inquiry through approvals, quotation, order status, invoice and payment exception; reuses existing CRM/ERP.
2. **Project Margin / Variations Recovery:** identify contractual scope changes from field/e-mail/RFI and prepare evidence for human approval; track unbilled work and margin at completion.
3. **Industrial Procurement Exceptions:** BOM normalization, vendor quote checks, approval queue, stock/lead-time and missing invoice/delivery exceptions.

Go to market with **specific buyer KPI and signed monthly operating role**, not with an unspecified AI-consulting slogan. These offers can be specialized to energy, manufacturing and distribution and may be sold with 1C/Bitrix implementers as delivery partners.

## Next primary market test

1. Mystery-shop / request scoping estimates ethically with **truthful context** from 8–12 providers: YYTECH, A2, Whale, Novacom Systems, First Bit, Chota, JS Global, Proresult, Mobecan, R9. Ask 1 identical anonymized business problem: 200 technical RFQs/month; 1C + mailbox + CRM; estimate baseline unknown; human approval and exception handling mandatory. Record scope, price, delivery, exclusions, SLA, data/on-prem and IP ownership.
2. Interview **12 buyers** from manufacturing / industrial distribution / EPC: name last actual lost or delayed quote, unbilled change, late supplier payment; actual price paid for existing software/integrator.
3. Compare *delivered scope*, not headline price.
4. Qualify first paid audit only when baseline, loss and decision-maker known.
5. Partner track: talk to 1C/Bitrix/ECM integrators, especially those without comparable closed-loop offerings.

## Evidence/verification boundaries
- Own site descriptions, client counts and ROI are vendor claims, not verified market shares.
- External Directum project report and 1C partner/implementation directory strengthen proof of actual implementation; still do not prove a specific AI competitor's revenue or margin.
- This scan makes **no exhaustive Belarus company count**, no census-based share claim and no statement that an unobserved managed service does not exist.
- Entity note: `PRORESULT / bit24.by` treated as a single connected offer family for conservative deduplication; business legal relationship should be confirmed before formal competitor due diligence.
