# Cross-vertical market scan — where the money might be

Date: 2026-09-29. Market geography for first validation: Belarus; Russia/other jurisdictions only after local product, tax, legal and channel validation.

## Important distinction

Industrial production value, trade turnover and AI adoption rates **are not** the addressable spend on an automation product. Buyer-level WTP, procurement path, integration cost and support burden must be determined in interviews and pilots.

External directional evidence:
1. McKinsey, State of AI 2026: surveyed organizations most frequently report cost reductions in supply chain management, service operations and manufacturing; revenue gains most frequently in marketing/sales and product/service development. International self-reported study, not Belarus sample: https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai
2. McKinsey, AEC, 2026-07-15: meaningful potential in project workflows, RFI/submittal routing, cost forecast, change orders and document control. The quoted 39% of nonphysical work is a modeled *potential*, not achieved automation: https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/engineering-construction-and-building-materials/our-insights/how-ai-is-reshaping-the-future-of-the-aec-industry
3. McKinsey, distribution, 2026-06-09: US distributor survey identifies logistics and inventory among AI/analytics impact areas; geography is US, not Belarus: https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/industrials/our-insights/where-value-is-won-and-lost-in-distribution
4. McKinsey, COO manufacturing survey (2025; >$1bn revenue manufacturers): larger industrial firms report increasing digital/AI plans; it does not prove SMB WTP: https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/from-pilots-to-performance-how-coos-can-scale-ai-in-manufacturing
5. OECD, Italian finance survey (2026): AI adoption among insurance and banking respondents is substantial, but this does not transfer to Belarus SMB pricing: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/artificial-intelligence-in-italian-financial-markets_6f42c977-en.html
6. Eurostat Digitalisation 2026: 2025 EU AI adoption 55% at large enterprises vs 19% SMEs. The difference is relevant for buying capacity and digital readiness but is not Belarus data: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/interactive-publications/digitalisation-2026
7. 2025 Belarus sector turnover reported from Belstat: industrial output around 208 billion BYN and wholesale turnover 180.2 billion BYN. This is context for value-flow size, NOT market size for our product: https://ru.belsat.eu/91084998/belstat-dannye-2025
8. IEA, 2026: data-centre electricity demand rose 17% in 2025; large-load grid readiness is a strategic high-ticket engineering niche, not a volume SMB SaaS market: https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai

## Opportunity approach: vertical wedge + horizontal kernel

The strongest transferable *commercial hypothesis* is not generic AI consulting. Use the same reusable process kernel across:
- `intake/RFQ → technical offer → CRM`;
- `request → supplier quote → purchasing → ERP`;
- `field evidence → project cost/claim → document package`;
- `invoice → bank → accounting reconciliation → review queue`;
- `source/version → knowledge → actionable compliance work`.

Go to market as **Industrial B2B Operations + AI**, initially using energy/electrical/manufacturing language where founder has trust, then move one step outward to machinery, building materials, packaging, distribution and logistics. Reusing kernel does not mean bypassing industry-specific process design.

## Commercial conclusion (hypothesis, to be tested)

- **Accessible value pool to test first:** manufacturers, B2B industrial distributors, construction/EPC and industrial services. They have multiple processes with quantifiable money leakage and buyers who can authorize a bounded audit/pilot.
- **Highest potential contract size but difficult first sales:** banks, insurers, large utilities, critical infrastructure, data centres. Do not make Year 1 burn dependent on signing them.
- **Low-ticket hazard:** micro-ETL/standalone electrofacility compliance SaaS at 50–100 BYN/mon without a scalable distributor network. A white-label or managed package may be viable instead.
- **Founder role:** discovery, business case, enterprise relationship, product owner and delivery acceptance. AI coding/engineering specialists are delegated, with human engineering/legal approvals.

## Validation: interview before investment

Interview 18 companies (3 manufacturing, 3 distributors, 3 EPC/construction, 3 industrial service, 3 logistics/warehouse, 3 energy-enterprise/adjacent). Track:
- last actual costly workflow, volume, time, exceptions;
- existing ERP/CRM/1C/API and actual budget owner;
- baseline and hard vs capacity-only savings;
- whether they will share data / sponsor paid diagnosis;
- willingness to pay (quote test, not hypothetical «интересно»);
- existing vendors and procurement path;
- sales cycle and gross margin after implementation/support.

First scope of work should be one bounded process; avoid replacing the core system or handling live OT commands.
