# Belarus: expanded competitor and channel map (56 identified provider profiles)

**Research cut-off: 2026-09-29.** This is commercial-scope desk research, not a Belarus legal-entity census, market-share estimate, quality rating or proof that all listed suppliers offer agentic AI.

## Executive result — avoid adding incomparable numbers

**56 relevant named provider/offer profiles in our manually selected register**:
- 28 explicitly market AI, workflow, BPM and/or agent solutions;
- 28 are adjacent ERP/CRM/ECM/BPM/industrial/finance incumbents or value-chain providers.

This extends the previous **33** by **23** screened profiles. Registry is `COMPETITORS_BY_56_2026-09-29.json`. At least one provider (Algonix) appears to be an independent specialist rather than a separate confirmed incorporated company; accordingly the unit is a *supplier profile / offer family*, **not** verified legal entities. We counted the PRORESULT/bit24.by linked offer conservatively as one. We did not count a vendor brand and its local integrator twice.

**External checks, not additive to 56:**
- [Wadline AI Automation Belarus](https://wadline.com/ai-automation/by) reports **61 directory entries on 2026-09-28** (page refreshed 28 Sept); its category contains a broader set of SEO/web/offshore/generic software firms. Neither “56+61” nor “Belarus has exactly 61 dedicated AI studios” is justified.
- [Official 1C Belarus ERP Competence Directory](https://1c.by/firm1c/partner/erp/) shows **5 centres and 5 candidates**, partly overlapping the above registry. These are important established enterprise-data channels, not automatically LLM experts.
- [Official Bitrix24 partner directory](https://www.bitrix24.com/partners/) further broadens the supply side; no reproducible Belarus-only partner count was exposed in this pass, so none has been invented.

## Competitor-price and sales implications

**Published narrow entry points, NOT apples-to-apples enterprise implementation quotes:**
- [ЯРЧЕ n8n](https://yarche.by/automation/n8n): simple workflow pilot from **690 BYN**, multi-flow setup from **1,490 BYN**.
- [R9 BPM](https://r9.by/business-process-automation): **900 / 1,800 / 3,600 BYN from** for different advertised packages.
- [Deadline AI](https://deadline.by/uslugi/ai): RAG bot from **2,000 BYN**, AI agent from **3,500 BYN**.
- [YYTECH](https://yytech.by/): AI audit 1–2 weeks and pilot 4–6 weeks (duration claim, no comparable price).

Therefore the earlier **10–12k BYN audit** is a *test quote for a serious scoped engagement*, requiring two–three actual processes, data review, verified baseline, business case, architecture/risks, pilot and acceptance plan. It is not the default starting fee in Belarus and may not clear SME willingness-to-pay.

## Revised hypothesis worth testing

Do not brand as “AI automates companies” or “we uniquely process tender PDF.” Compete as a vendor-neutral **Industrial Operations / Profit Leakage Partner**:
1. technical inquiry → verified BOM/cost → quote approval → order/ERP status → invoice/payment exception;
2. field evidence → contract change → review/approval → unbilled extras → project margin forecast;
3. procurement requests → multi-vendor quotations → substitutions/lead time → approvals → reconciliation;
4. multi-site equipment/compliance exceptions → engineer-owned SLA and audited evidence.

Offer a paid, bounded outcome and an ongoing **managed function** only if buyer will pay for actual handling, exception ownership and reporting. Look for non-exclusive technical partnerships with 1C/ECM/BPM/OT vendors.

## Next competitive intelligence cycle

### Seller side
Contact 8–12 providers with **truthful buyer-independent scope research**, not false customer identities. Use [common brief](COMPETITOR_COMPARISON_BRIEF.md):
- S1 = PDF/email→human-reviewed RFQ/quote draft;
- S2 = S1+cost/stock/CRM/ERP/invoice/payment exception loop;
- S3 = managed operation, support/SLA and responsibility for exception queue.

Record setup fee, exclusions, monthly run costs, licenses, data/on-prem, implementation references, IP, processing permissions, SLA and support. Do not pretend that price on S1 competes with S2/S3.

### Buyer side
12 interviews in manufacturing, distributors and EPC: who signed last automation contract; price actually paid; last missed quote, unbilled contract change, cost error, long approval; decision-maker and real budget. Gap in marketing is not gap in market.
